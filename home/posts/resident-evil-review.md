Well after having spent a few decades watching cheesy Resident Evil
movies, I went in to this one with absolutely zero expectations. I
also usually avoid trailers and reviews. This movie is great! I did
not expect it would also be funny. The monsters are wonderfully done
and there are some truly uncomfortable moments, and the pace never
slows down.

It is great to see the Horror genre blossoming this decade.
Recommended, 9/10. I'm not saying this is high art but it's just pure
fun.
